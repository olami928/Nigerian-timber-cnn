Getting a useful image

How to photograph

The model reads the visible structure of the cut end. A straight-on, sharp, evenly lit photograph gives it the best chance of a useful result.

  1. Show the cut end-grain

    Photograph the transverse (cross-section) surface, not the side of the plank.

  2. Use a flat, clean cut

    Brush away loose dust without covering the wood surface or filling the pores.

  3. Hold steady and focus

    Use good, even light and wait for the surface detail to appear sharp before taking the photo.

  4. Fill the frame with wood

    Keep fingers, tools, and unrelated objects out of the frame where possible.

What to avoid

  • Side-grain, bark, or angled faces instead of the cut end.
  • Motion blur, heavy shadow, glare, or a wet, dusty surface.
  • Very small images where the grain cannot be seen.

Research prototype

About and limits

A machine-learning study of Nigerian timber species from end-grain photographs, with documented uses shown only from the literature.

Current status

On 611 independent-specimen test images from 14 species, image-level accuracy was 12.1% for ShuffleNetV2 and 11.3% for ResNet18. Fifteen single-specimen species had no independent-specimen test result.

Low independent-specimen accuracy means this research prototype is not a validated timber identification service.

What it covers

This project covers 29 timber species traded in Ibadan wood markets, using photographs of transverse (end-grain) surfaces. It cannot identify species outside this scope or recognise softwoods.

What the percentage means

The confidence percentage represents patch agreement: the share of the 48 image sections that voted for the displayed species. It is not a probability or a guarantee that the identification is correct.

Documented uses

Any uses shown are documented literature uses associated with the identified species; they are not predicted from the photograph. Species with no documented use show no uses section.

Privacy and appropriate use

Images are processed locally in your browser and are not uploaded or stored by this site. The model file is downloaded from this site for local inference.

This prototype is not intended for legal, import/export, purchasing, or structural-grading decisions.
